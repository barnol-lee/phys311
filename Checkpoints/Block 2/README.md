The engine demo contained in this folder has in it the architecture for many distinct physics simulations. By relying on the architecture, one can add new bodies or new forces to their simulation with a simple add function, add collision constraints pertaining to walls or between bodies by passing it through the world class, and one can record relevant variables with world.record. Then, the whole trouble of running the simulation is put together in the world.run function.
The specific demo I gave has 4 particles, 3 free and 1 fixed, wherein they are connected by springs in sequence. The system functions as a triple pendulum, with gravity, springs, and collisons. One can see as well that the energy of the system floats around its inital value, pointing to oscillations being from the Euler-Cromer integrator rather than any error per se.
